Appearance

Style
SizeScales every black hole. Scroll on one to change just its mass.
TiltHow far the disk is tipped toward you.
GlowLight bleeding around the brightest parts, as it does in a camera. Off skips that work entirely.
Einstein ringA distant star exactly behind the black hole, bent into a ring of light.
Motion blurSmears it along its path when it moves fast.

Light

Colour gradeHow the light is coloured, like a camera's picture style. The desktop behind isn't changed.
Shadow on what's behindA soft shadow under the disk on the windows and desktop behind it.
Blueprint viewDraws the paths light takes around each black hole, with the horizon, photon sphere, shadow edge and innermost stable orbit marked. Red paths fall in.
Doppler colourGas coming toward you looks bluer and hotter, gas going away redder, as it really would.

Accretion disk

ColourThe glow of the cooler gas. Hot spots still burn toward white.
TemperatureHotter gas is brighter and whiter.
ThicknessHeight of the gas compared with its distance out. Real thin disks are about 1–10%. A thicker disk shades its own outer parts.
Orbit speedThe inner disk always laps the outer disk.

Black hole

Spin0 doesn't spin. Higher spin drags light around one side, flattening the shadow, and lets the disk come closer. Feeding spins it up.

SoundA low hum, a gulp when it feeds, a chirp when two merge, and the burst when it evaporates. It sits left or right with where the black holes are.
Volume
Sound themeSonification: a deep, slowly drifting chord pitched to the black hole's mass (heavier is lower), like NASA's sonifications of real black holes.
Dance to music on the PCWhen music plays in any app, black holes sway to the beat. The PC's sound is only listened to for the beat, live; nothing is recorded.
Play with windowsWhen it's bored and sitting next to a small window, it bats it a few pixels and back. Never the window you're using, a maximised one, or anything big.
Slowly evaporate when unfedAfter three days without food, a black hole loses about 2% of its mass a day (Hawking radiation, sped up a great deal). It never goes below its starting size.

Hold it over a window for a moment, then let go: it swallows the window. Right-click to give windows back.
