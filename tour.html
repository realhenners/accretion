This is your black hole

It sits on top of your screen and bends everything behind it, like a real one. Nothing you see it do changes your files or windows.

Drag it to move it. Let go while moving to throw it. Scroll on it to make it heavier or lighter.

↓

Feed it

Hold it still over a window for a moment, then let go: it swallows the window. Or drag things onto it: files, folders, pictures, text, a link.

Nothing is lost. Right-click it to spit things back out, or press Ctrl+Alt+Z to undo the last meal.

♡

It's a pet

It gets hungry, bored and sleepy, and it has moods. Slowly circle the cursor round its edge to pet it. Rest the cursor on it to see how it's feeling.

Feeding it spins it up and makes it grow. Look after it and it may have babies.

⋯

Right-click for everything else

Name it, make it stay put, share it, change how it looks, and see everything it's eaten and done (its journal) in Settings.

Some things are hidden. Try things and see what happens.
